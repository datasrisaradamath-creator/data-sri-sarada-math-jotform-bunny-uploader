How to fill this section

  • Mandatory fields (marked * in red): First Name, Surname, Gender, and Standard. Middle Name and Division are optional.
  • Click Add Student at the bottom of the section to add another student row. You can add up to 25 students.
  • To remove a student, click the dustbin button at the right edge of that student's row.
You won't be able to proceed to the next page if any added student row has a mandatory field left blank.
