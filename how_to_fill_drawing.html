How to fill this section

This competition has two parts: a Student section (singers) and an optional Musician section (instrumentalists).

Students (required)
  • Mandatory fields (marked * in red): First Name, Surname, Gender, and Standard. Middle Name and Division are optional.
  • Up to 10 students can be entered. Click + Add Student to add a row. Click Delete Student at the right edge to remove a row.
  • Each student must be unique — two rows with the same First Name, Surname, Gender and Standard will be flagged as duplicates and must be resolved before you can proceed.
Musicians (optional)
  • Musicians are optional — leave the section empty if you have none.
  • Up to 2 musicians can be entered. Click + Add Musician to add a row. Click Delete Musician at the right edge to remove a row.
You won't be able to proceed if any student row has a mandatory field left blank or if duplicate student entries are detected. Either complete the row or click Delete Student to remove it.
