How to fill this section

  • Mandatory fields (marked * in red): First Name, Surname, Gender and Standard. Middle Name and Division are optional.
  • Click + Add Student to add a row. You can add up to 25 students.
  • To remove a student, click Delete Student at the right edge of that row.
  • Each student must be unique — two rows with the same First Name, Surname, Gender and Standard are not allowed.
  • You won't be able to proceed if any student row has a mandatory field left blank or if duplicate entries are detected.
