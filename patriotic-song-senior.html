How to fill this section

This competition has two parts: a Student section (singers) and an optional Musician section (instrumentalists).

Students (required)
  • Mandatory fields (marked * in red): First Name, Surname, Gender and Standard. Middle Name and Division are optional.
  • Click + Add Student to add a row (up to 10 students). Click Delete Student at the right edge to remove a row.
  • Each student must be unique — two rows with the same First Name, Surname, Gender and Standard are not allowed.
Musicians (optional)
  • Optional — leave the section empty if you have none (up to 2 musicians).
  • If you add a musician, fill all required fields: First Name, Surname, Participant (Student/Teacher), Gender and Standard (teachers can pick NA). Middle Name and Division are optional.

You can't proceed if a student row has a mandatory field blank, if students are duplicated, or if a musician row is started but missing a required field.
